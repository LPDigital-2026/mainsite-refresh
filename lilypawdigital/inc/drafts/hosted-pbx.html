The UK's old copper phone network is being switched off, and traditional business phone lines are going with it. Openreach has been moving everyone onto digital lines, with the final switch-off due by January 2027. If your business still relies on an old phone system, now is a good time to look at a hosted PBX.

What is a PBX?

A PBX (private branch exchange) is the system that runs a business phone setup: extensions, transferring calls, hunt groups, voicemail, call queues, opening hours and on-hold music. Traditionally it was a box on the office wall. A hosted PBX does the same job from a server in a data centre, and your phones connect to it over the internet.

Why businesses switch

  • Your number goes with you. Staff can answer calls on a desk phone, a laptop or a mobile app, in the office or at home.
  • No box to maintain. There is no hardware in the office to fail, and changes can be made in minutes.
  • It grows with you. Adding a new extension or a new office is a setting, not an engineer visit.
  • Ready for the digital switch-off. It already works the way all UK phone lines will soon have to.

FreePBX, FusionPBX and the rest

There are several proven phone systems to choose from. FreePBX is one of the most widely used, with a friendly web interface that suits most small and medium businesses. FusionPBX is built for flexibility and handles several companies or sites on one system well. Others suit more specialised needs. With a hosted PBX from us you choose the system that fits, and it runs on a dedicated server with its own processing power, memory, storage and daily backups.

Can I keep my phone number?

In most cases, yes. UK numbers can usually be moved, or "ported", from your current provider to a new one. New numbers, including local Newcastle and North East numbers, are also available on request.

What you need

  • A reliable internet connection at each site
  • Phones: desk handsets, softphone apps on computers and mobiles, or a mixture
  • A plan for your numbers: keep the ones you have, add new ones, or both
