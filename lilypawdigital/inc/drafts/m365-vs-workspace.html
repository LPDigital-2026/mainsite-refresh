For most small businesses, the choice of email and office software comes down to two options: Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace. Both are excellent, both give you email on your own domain, and both keep your files in the cloud. The right one depends on how your team works. Here is a plain-English comparison.

What they have in common

  • Professional email on your own domain
  • Online file storage and sharing
  • Calendars, video calls and chat
  • Documents, spreadsheets and presentations
  • Access from computers, phones and tablets
  • Security features, including multi-factor authentication

Where Microsoft 365 stands out

  • The familiar desktop apps. Business Standard and above include the full desktop versions of Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook. If your team lives in Excel, or you swap Word documents with customers and suppliers, this matters.
  • Outlook. Many people simply prefer Outlook for email and calendars, especially on a computer.
  • Teams. Chat, calls and meetings in one place, widely used by larger organisations you may work with.
  • Device management and security. Business Premium adds advanced threat protection and tools to manage company laptops and phones.

Where Google Workspace stands out

  • Browser first. Gmail, Docs and Sheets are built to work in a web browser, with nothing to install.
  • Real-time collaboration. Several people editing the same document at once has always been Google's strength, although Microsoft has largely caught up.
  • Simplicity. Fewer options can mean less to learn, which suits some small teams.

Questions to help you decide

  • Do your customers and suppliers send you Word and Excel files?
  • Does your team prefer desktop apps or working in a browser?
  • Do you need to manage and secure company laptops?
  • Are you already using one of them in some way?

If you answered yes to the first or third question, Microsoft 365 is usually the better fit. If your team works mostly in the browser and keeps things simple, Google Workspace can work well.

Can you switch later?

Yes. Email, files and calendars can be moved between the two, although it takes planning. It is much easier to choose well at the start.
