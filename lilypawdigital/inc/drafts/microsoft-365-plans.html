Microsoft 365 comes in three main business plans: Business Basic, Business Standard and Business Premium. They share the same core, so the question is not which one is best, but which one your business actually needs. Here is the difference in plain English.

What every plan includes

  • Professional email on your own domain, with a 50 GB mailbox per user
  • Teams for chat, calls and video meetings
  • 1 TB of OneDrive storage per user, plus SharePoint for shared files
  • Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook in a web browser and on phones and tablets

Business Basic: email, Teams and storage

Basic suits teams that mostly work in email and a browser. You get everything above, but not the installed desktop versions of Word, Excel and PowerPoint. If your staff already have Office on their computers, or rarely do more than read and edit documents, Basic is often all you need.

Business Standard: the full desktop apps

Standard adds the installed desktop apps, which each user can install on up to five PCs or Macs, five tablets and five phones. It also adds webinar tools and Bookings, which lets customers book appointments with you online. This is the plan most small offices choose, because the desktop apps are what people expect to use every day.

Business Premium: Standard plus security

Premium includes everything in Standard, plus Microsoft's business security tools:

  • Defender for Business, which protects laptops and computers against malware and ransomware
  • Intune, which manages your devices: you can enforce passwords and encryption, and wipe a lost laptop remotely
  • Conditional access, which controls who can sign in, from where and on which devices

Premium makes sense if you handle sensitive client information, have staff working from home on company laptops, or need to show insurers or customers that your devices are managed and protected.

How to choose

  • Email and Teams, mostly in a browser: Business Basic
  • You want the full Office apps on your computers: Business Standard
  • You also need devices managed and properly protected: Business Premium

You can also mix plans, giving Standard to office staff and Basic to people who only need email. Plans can be changed later as your needs grow.
