What Is Colocation?

Colocation means you own and manage your own server hardware, but keep it in a professional data centre instead of at home or in the office.

The facility provides secure rack space, resilient power, cooling and fast connectivity, while you stay in full control of your own equipment. It is a good fit if you want the control of your own hardware without the cost of running a server room yourself.

What Can You Use It For?

Anything you would run on your own hardware. Here are a few common examples.

Your Own Servers

Move hardware out of the office or home and into a secure, properly powered and cooled facility.

Websites & Web Apps

Host your own sites, applications and databases on hardware you control.

Private Cloud

Run virtualisation platforms and build your own private cloud on your own kit.

Backups & Recovery

Keep an off-site copy of your data, away from your main premises.

Network Equipment

Rack your own routers, firewalls and switches close to fast, diverse connectivity.

Game & Media Servers

Run game servers, streaming or media platforms with reliable power and fast connectivity.
