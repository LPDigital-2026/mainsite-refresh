Frequently Asked Questions

Something else you need to know? Get in touch and we will answer it.

What is cPanel?

cPanel is the most widely used web hosting control panel in the world. It gives you a simple dashboard to manage your websites, email, databases, files and one-click app installs.

What does Imunify360 do?

Imunify360 is a security suite that protects every account on the server. It combines a firewall, malware scanning and protection against common attacks, so problems are caught and blocked automatically.

Can you move my website from my current host?

Yes, and it is free. We move your websites, email and databases for you, with as little downtime as possible.

Is WordPress supported?

Absolutely. Install WordPress in one click with Softaculous, and enjoy LiteSpeed caching tuned for WordPress, with the PHP version you choose.

How much power does each plan get?

Every plan gets 2 CPU cores and 2GB of RAM, on the latest enterprise-grade servers with dual CPUs, 48 or more cores and at least 128GB of RAM, so there is plenty of headroom.

Are there any setup fees?

No. There are no setup fees and no hidden extras. You pay the monthly price shown for your plan.
