Web Design Questions

What's included in the free 30-day trial?

We build your site on a demo URL and it is live for 30 days at no cost. If you love it, it is just £99 to keep it live, plus hosting from £5.75 a month. If it is not for you, you can walk away. No cost and no obligation.

Do I need to know anything technical?

Not at all. We handle the design, the build, and the hosting. If you ever need something changed once your site is live, just get in touch and we will sort it.

Is hosting included?

Yes. Every plan includes managed hosting on real UK-based hardware, so you never have to deal with a separate hosting provider.

What if I need changes after my site is live?

Just get in touch and we will sort it. Whether it is a question or something you would like adjusted, that is what ongoing support is there for.

Which package is right for my business?

If you are not sure, get in touch and tell us what you are after. We will point you toward the right plan, or put together something custom if nothing quite fits.

How We're Different

A few things that set us apart, explained simply.

Fair, Transparent Pricing

You will always know exactly what you are paying and why. No hidden fees, no surprise charges, and no contracts designed to catch you out. Just a clear price for a service that works.

We Actually Care

Everything we build and run gets real attention from a real person, not a template filled in and forgotten. We are a small North East England business, and everything we do has our name on it, so we make sure it is done properly.

Your Site Is Secured

Every site comes with an SSL certificate as standard. In simple terms, this puts a padlock next to your website address and encrypts the information moving between your visitors and your site, so things like contact form details stay private.

Protected Against Attacks

Your site is protected against DDoS attacks. This is when someone tries to knock a website offline by flooding it with fake traffic. Our protection filters that traffic out, so your site stays up and running as normal.
