=== Lily Paw SEO ===
Version: 1.0.3

Sitemaps, page titles and descriptions, link previews and indexing rules for lilypaw.digital.

What it does
- Sitemap at /sitemap.xml (pages, articles with their pictures, categories), kept up to date automatically. robots.txt points to it. WordPress's own /wp-sitemap.xml is switched off and redirects here.
- A title and description for every page. The site's pages come with suggested ones; change any of them in the "Search results (SEO)" box under the page content. Articles use their excerpt.
- Link-preview tags (Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, X) with the right picture.
- Keeps thin pages out of Google: tag, author and date archives, search results and attachment pages. Author pages (which reveal your username) redirect to About Us.
- "Hide this page from search engines" switch on every page and article.
- Settings → SEO shows the sitemap address, how to submit it to Google and Bing, and every page's title and description with any problems flagged.

Install: Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin, choose lilypaw-seo.zip, Activate.
Works with the Lily Paw Digital theme, which steps aside for it. Don't run it alongside another SEO plugin (Yoast, Rank Math and so on).
